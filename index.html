Adrian William Romero Jorge

Ph.D. Researcher at Goethe University Frankfurt. I work on theoretical physics, heavy-ion collisions, and dark matter.

View papers View talks Beyond Physics View Art

Research interests

  • High-energy nuclear physics
  • Heavy-ion collisions
  • Transport theory and microscopic simulations
  • Dilepton production in nuclear collisions
  • Dark matter phenomenology
  • Dark sector physics
  • Vector-portal models and dark photons
  • Cosmological and astrophysical constraints on dark matter
