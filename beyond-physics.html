Beyond Physics 03

Xima (board game & Scientific Xima)

I am a co-founder of Xima, a Cuban 3D science board game project. This section combines the game vision, rules highlights, and my book Scientific Xima where a new dynamical method to calculate force and power of the game was implemented.

Official Website Play Xima Watch Videos Scientific Xima Web Download Scientific Xima (PDF)

Goal of the Game

Bring your special block to the top of the board before your opponents.

How to Play?

Each player starts with 10 blocks and, on every turn, can either add a new block or move one already on the board.

New gamemode Scienluck

Xima can be played as pure strategy, with chance (dice), or in a hybrid format that combines both approaches.

new CIMAX system

The book introduces game force, game power, game level, and CIMAX to quantify performance, strategy, and progression.
